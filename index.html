FAQ

Common questions

How long will my bike be with you?

Punctures and small adjustments are usually done the same day. Services take one to two working days, depending on parts.

Do you work on electric bikes?

Yes, for brakes, gears, wheels and tyres. We do not repair motors or batteries.

Can I bring my own parts?

You can. We charge the normal labour price and check the parts are compatible before fitting them.

What if the quote is more than I want to spend?

Just say no. The check is free, and we will tell you which jobs matter most for safety.
